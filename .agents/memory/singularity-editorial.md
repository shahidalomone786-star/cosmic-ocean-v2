---
name: Singularity editorial system
description: Durable rules for the response reading experience and AI editorial behavior.
---

Singularity treats response presentation as an editorial surface: rendered content uses a narrow reading measure with generous rhythm, restrained tables/callouts/code, and unchanged math parsing. Editorial writing guidance is appended to the existing scientific system prompt rather than replacing persona, truthfulness, or adaptive reasoning rules.

**Why:** The product should feel like a calm research platform instead of a generic chatbot, while preserving the established transport and scientific reliability behavior.

**How to apply:** Keep future Markdown refinements in the renderer, keep LaTeX behavior compatible, and add new writing guidance as a clearly scoped prompt extension. Prefer natural structure over forced headings or templates.
